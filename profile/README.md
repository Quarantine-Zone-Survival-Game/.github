## 🧠 Overview

**Quarantine Zone: The Last Check** is a tense survival-focused simulation game that places players in control of a critical inspection checkpoint during the collapse of civilization. Set in a dangerous quarantine environment overwhelmed by infection and uncertainty, the game challenges players to make difficult decisions that directly impact the fate of survivors attempting to cross the final secure border.

Players take on the role of a checkpoint officer responsible for inspecting civilians, identifying potential threats, managing limited resources, and preventing infected individuals from entering protected zones. Every interaction carries consequences, forcing players to balance humanity, caution, and survival under constant pressure.

The gameplay combines strategic decision-making with inspection mechanics, environmental management, and survival-based resource control. Players must carefully examine documents, detect suspicious behavior, monitor symptoms, and react quickly to escalating situations while maintaining security and public order.

As conditions deteriorate, moral dilemmas and unpredictable encounters become increasingly complex. Civilians may hide injuries, attempt to smuggle resources, or desperately seek safety despite possible infection. Each decision shapes the progression of the quarantine zone and influences the survival of both individuals and the wider community.

The game features atmospheric visual design, immersive tension-building audio, and realistic survival themes that reinforce the feeling of operating within a collapsing world. Dynamic encounters and evolving scenarios ensure that every shift at the checkpoint feels unpredictable and emotionally intense.

Whether screening survivors, managing emergency situations, or making impossible ethical decisions under pressure, **Quarantine Zone: The Last Check** delivers a gripping survival simulation experience for Windows players.
